## Operating principle

The 2027 Derby field will be selected from the official Road to the Kentucky Derby points system. Horses earn points by finishing top-5 in designated prep races. The top 20 point-earners (plus 4 also-eligibles from international roads) start.

Goal of this tracker: **Identify the 2027 Golden Tempo by January.** Build the watch list early, score against the framework as preps unfold, and be 5 weeks ahead of the public on the actual Derby horses.

## Strategy: signals to track

1. **Curlin/Tapit/Maxfield/Quality Road sons** — auto-add to watch list when they break their maiden
2. **Show finishes in G2/G3 preps with positive trip notes** — most underbet profile
3. **Trainers with multiple Derby entries last 3 years** — DeVaux, Cox, Mott, Pletcher, C. Brown, Baffert
4. **Late developers** — horses making first start in March/April with strong figures from one race
5. **Japanese imports** — Japan Road horses still searching for first Derby win, but trend is upward
